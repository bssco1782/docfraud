Notes
------------------------------------------------------------
  - A 'hit' needs only an intersection with the edit rectangle; IoU shows how tight the box is. Ink boxes cover the inserted words, which are smaller than the edit's white box, so their IoU is low even when correct.
  - A text overlay is found through its ink (a run of glyphs with a darkness level rare on the page) or, on tinted paper, through its white box; where the page's own text shares the overlay's darkness level, neither signal fires.
  - Regions describe where the page is anomalous, not proof of an edit; synthetic data only.
