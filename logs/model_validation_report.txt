EDGE CASES AND FAILURE SCENARIOS
--------------------------------
- Demo: 1/5 images wrong: tampered_payslip_02.png (tampered -> genuine).
- TEST: 2/10 genuine flagged (FPR 20.00%), 0/5 tampered missed (FNR 0.00%).
- HOLDOUT: 1/83 genuine flagged (FPR 1.20%), 10/66 tampered missed (FNR 15.15%).
- Missed edits by what they left on the page: visible local edit 5, no pixel changed (edit on blank paper) 3, only the inserted words (white box on white paper) 2.
- Invalid uploads are rejected, not crashed: corrupt.jpg, truncated demo PNG (first 50% of bytes), empty file, text file named .png.
- Off-distribution inputs (non-document fixtures, small, grayscale, large) still get a score: clean_rgb.jpg 0.62, clean_rgb.png 0.62, grayscale.jpg 0.62, large_image.jpg 0.55, small_image.jpg 0.60, tampered_patch.jpg 0.42. The model has no 'not a document' output; such scores are not meaningful.
- Re-saved, resized or re-exposed copies are harder: cross-validation on processed copies of the training documents gives lower accuracy than on pristine files (logs/training_log_v2.txt; logs/model_stability_report.txt section 6).

--------------------------------
SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------------------
- The accuracy requirement is met on synthetic data (HOLDOUT 92.62%). The next limit is real documents: collect a labelled set of real (consented or public-sample) payslips, statements and IDs and measure before any production use.
- Robustness to re-saving: processed copies are classified less accurately than pristine files, and JPEG is the weakest case. Score tiles relative to the page's own compression level (estimated JPEG quality) instead of absolute levels, and widen the processing used in training.
- Blank-paper edits: some generated 'tampered' documents change no pixel. Fix the generator to place edits on content (or record the edit box) so labels match what is on the page; report detection only on edits that changed pixels.
- Localisation: flagged_regions now boxes the anomalous region for flagged documents (logs/localization_report.txt); it misses most text overlays on bank statements, where the inserted words share the page's ink level.
- Keep the evaluation clean: HOLDOUT and TEST have now been used once for v2. A future model needs a new holdout set (new seed) so it is not selected on these.
- Calibrate the score and add a 'needs review' band around the threshold, chosen on validation data.
- Pin scikit-learn, numpy, OpenCV, Pillow and SciPy, record the model SHA-256 per release, and re-run scripts/model_stability_test.py and scripts/model_validation.py after every dependency or model change.
