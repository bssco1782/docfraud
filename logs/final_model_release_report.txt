SECTION 4: KNOWN LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Synthetic data only: one generator, three templates (payslip, bank statement, ID proof), four
    edit types. Accuracy on real documents has never been measured.
  - Held-out accuracy by document type: bank_statement 83.02% (44/53), id_proof 96.36% (53/55), payslip 96.43% (54/56).
  - Degraded pages (164 held-out documents, 92.07% unmodified): JPEG q85 73.17%,
    50% downscale 50.00%, 180 deg rotation 64.02%, 3 deg skew 70.12%, 2 px crop 79.88% (logs/model_robustness_report.txt).
  - Non-document images get a score and are often reported as tampered (Appendix C).
  - Scores are uncalibrated vote fractions; 4 held-out documents lie within 0.05 of the threshold.
  - Missed edits: blur on blank paper changes no pixel (undetectable); text overlays whose ink matches
    the page; some colour shifts. Text-replacement edits (demo tampered_payslip_02) were never trained.
  - flagged_regions: a box lands on 55/67 held-out edits; boxes are anomalies, not proof, and unreliable on resampled pages (logs/localization_report.txt).
  - Only page 1 of a PDF is analysed. One uvicorn process served 8.3 requests/s on 4 CPUs; multi-worker set-ups are unmeasured.
  Full list, edge cases, failure scenarios and future work: logs/model_limitations.txt
